◆ gateTypeFromPrimitiveName()

std::optional< GateType > atpg::ir::gateTypeFromPrimitiveName ( std::string_view name)

Maps a built-in SV gate primitive keyword (and/nand/or/nor/xor/xnor/buf/not) to its GateType. Returns std::nullopt for anything else, including "PI"/"PO" which are not real primitives.
